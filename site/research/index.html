How we work

Years 1 and 2 are research-led. We work with university and government research labs on joint testing, shared testbeds and co-authored papers. Every workstream produces a measured result rather than a claim, and every collaboration is also a route to the first users of Heleo Mesh.

Five Year 1 workstreams

WorkstreamWhat is doneOutput
1. RadiationBeam-test the selected low-power compute module at a proton or heavy-ion facilityMeasured error and latch-up rates, total dose tolerance
2. ShieldingCompare shielding options by mass against radiation reductionThe lightest shielding that meets the dose target
3. Fault-tolerant meshCombine the hardware supervisor with mesh-level recovery on the ground testbed, under injected faultsRecovery time and data-loss figures
4. Thermal-vacuumTest the compute module and radiator in a vacuum chamber; validate heat-aware schedulingVerified heat limits per node
5. Settlement under stressRun settlement between ground nodes while faults are injectedProof that payments stay correct when nodes fail

Risks we track openly

  • Thermal performance at high compute density.
  • Long-term memory degradation under radiation.
  • Inter-satellite link cost and timing.
  • Ground-station bandwidth.
  • Launch price: the business case depends on launch costs falling.

Open questions

  1. Which shielding mass gives the best cost per unit of radiation reduction for the chosen compute module?
  2. How much work can the mesh recover after a radiation event without losing settlement correctness?
  3. At what launch price per kilogram does an orbital node match a ground node on cost per unit of compute?

Collaborate

We are looking for labs with beam-test, thermal-vacuum or distributed-systems capability, and for researchers who want to work on these questions with us. Results are shared with research partners and published where we can.

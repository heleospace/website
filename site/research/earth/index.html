Research · Field 02

Send down the answer, not the whole image

Earth observation creates more data than ground stations can download. Heleo Mesh and Heleo-1 let you process it closer to the source and send down only what matters.

Desert and snowy mountains from orbit · NASA

Overview

Satellites capture far more imagery than their downlinks can carry, and most of it waits hours before anyone looks at it. Processing data in orbit and sending down only the result, a flood outline, a crop map, a ship position, can cut that delay and save scarce bandwidth.

Heleo-1 is a compute satellite, not an imaging satellite: it processes data that reaches it over its links. Today you can build and test the same pipelines on the ground mesh with any dataset you already use.

What you can do

Processing in orbit

Run detection, classification and compression close to the sensor and send down the answer instead of the raw image.

Climate and environment

Process long time series of land, ocean and atmosphere data on the ground mesh, with no cluster of your own.

Disaster response studies

Test how quickly a pipeline can turn a new image into an alert, and what it takes to make that reliable.

Agriculture, oceans and maritime

Crop health, coastal change, sea-surface conditions and vessel tracking, with ground and orbit workflows on one network.

Research questions we want to help answer

  1. How much downlink can onboard processing save, for each kind of product, without losing scientific value?
  2. How close do models running in orbit come to the same models on the ground, under orbital power and radiation limits?
  3. How short can the time from capture to alert become for floods, fires and other events?
  4. How should models trained on ground data be validated once they run in orbit?
  5. Which open formats and datasets make results from orbit easy for other researchers to reuse?

How a project runs

Every project starts on the ground and moves to orbit only when it is ready. You keep the same code and the same account at every stage.

StageStatusWhat happens
1. Ground meshAvailable nowBuild and run your processing pipeline on ground nodes, on archive or live data you already have.
2. Heleo-1 ground twinPlannedRun the pipeline under Heleo-1 power, heat and link limits to see what survives the move to orbit.
3. Heleo-1 in orbitPlannedRun the pipeline on Heleo-1 on data that reaches it over its links, and compare results and timing with the ground.

Orbital access and hosted experiments are planned and subject to mission approval, licensing and export rules.

What we provide, what you bring

We provide

  • Compute on Heleo Mesh for processing and model training
  • Access to the Heleo-1 ground twin
  • Help packaging pipelines for orbital limits
  • Measured timing and power for every orbital run

You bring

  • A research question and the datasets behind it
  • Your processing code and models
  • Ground-truth data to check results against
  • A plan to publish methods and results

Your team leads the work, owns its data and keeps the credit. Results that use Heleo Mesh or Heleo-1 are shared and cited, so the whole field moves faster.

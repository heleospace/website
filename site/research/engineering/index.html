Research · Field 05

Heavy simulation, on demand

Run simulation, digital twins and design verification on Heleo Mesh when you need them, and pay only for the work that runs.

The sun's first rays above Earth's limb · NASA

Overview

Engineering research comes in bursts: a design review, a grant deadline, a parameter sweep that needs a thousand runs by Friday. Buying a cluster for the peak leaves it idle the rest of the year. Heleo Mesh lets you burst onto the compute you need and stop when you are done.

If your work involves space systems, the Heleo-1 ground twin gives you a real testbed to check your models against.

What you can do

Simulation and digital twins

Fluid dynamics, structures, thermal models and digital twins of systems you are building.

Design and verification

Regression runs, hardware verification and large test suites, split across many nodes.

Burst compute for deadlines

Scale up for a grant cycle or a design review, then scale back to zero.

Pay only for what runs

Each job settles automatically when it finishes. No reservations, no idle machines.

Typical workloads

  1. Orbit and mission simulation, from launch to deorbit
  2. Thermal and structural models of spacecraft and instruments
  3. Monte Carlo and parameter sweeps that need thousands of runs
  4. Digital twins that compare live telemetry with a model
  5. Chip and system verification suites

How a project runs

Every project starts on the ground and moves to orbit only when it is ready. You keep the same code and the same account at every stage.

StageStatusWhat happens
1. Ground meshAvailable nowRun your simulations on ground nodes, with research credits for selected labs.
2. Heleo-1 ground twinPlannedUse the Heleo-1 ground twin to check models of space systems against a real testbed.
3. Heleo-1 in orbitPlannedCompare your models with telemetry from Heleo-1 in orbit.

Orbital access and hosted experiments are planned and subject to mission approval, licensing and export rules.

What we provide, what you bring

We provide

  • Compute on Heleo Mesh, with research credits for selected labs
  • Help porting and packaging your solver
  • Access to the Heleo-1 ground twin
  • Telemetry for checking space-system models

You bring

  • Your models, solvers and input data
  • An estimate of the compute you need
  • A named lead researcher
  • A plan to publish or share results

Your team leads the work, owns its data and keeps the credit. Results that use Heleo Mesh or Heleo-1 are shared and cited, so the whole field moves faster.

Apply

Tell us about your lab, your question and what you want to run, on the ground or in orbit. Engineering, aerospace and simulation research groups are a good fit.

Research · Field 01

Train on the ground. Test in orbit.

Compute for training, fine-tuning and inference on Heleo Mesh today, and a path to run your model on Heleo-1, our planned orbital data center.

Photo: İsmail Enes Ayhan, Unsplash

Overview

AI research needs compute that is easy to get and fair to pay for. Heleo Mesh routes each job to the hardware that fits it and settles automatically, so a lab pays only for the work that runs, with no cluster to buy or reserve.

Orbit adds a question no ground lab can answer alone: how do models behave when radiation flips bits, power is tight and heat can only leave as radiation? Heleo-1 is designed to let you measure that on real hardware, then bring the results back to the ground.

What you can do

Training and fine-tuning

Run training and fine-tuning jobs on the ground mesh, routed to the nodes that fit each job.

Inference at scale

Serve models for experiments, evaluations and research tools, priced per job rather than per reserved machine.

AI under space conditions

Measure how models and accelerators respond to radiation, strict power budgets and heat limits, on the ground twin first and then in orbit.

Agents on the Mesh

Give research agents a Mesh ID so they can request compute, pay for it and report results on their own. About agents.

Research questions we want to help answer

  1. How much does accuracy drop when radiation causes bit flips in weights and activations, and which mitigations cost least?
  2. Can training and inference be checkpointed and moved between nodes fast enough to survive a node failure without losing work?
  3. What is the energy cost per result for common models under a strict orbital power budget?
  4. How should a model in orbit be updated when uplink time and bandwidth are limited?
  5. How should autonomous agents price, buy and verify the compute they use?

How a project runs

Every project starts on the ground and moves to orbit only when it is ready. You keep the same code and the same account at every stage.

StageStatusWhat happens
1. Ground meshAvailable nowRun training, fine-tuning and inference on ground nodes. Research credits are available for selected labs.
2. Heleo-1 ground twinPlannedRun the same workload on a testbed that mirrors the Heleo-1 compute pod, including its power and heat limits.
3. Heleo-1 in orbitPlannedRun your model on Heleo-1 in a dawn-dusk orbit at about 550 km and compare the results with the ground.

Orbital access and hosted experiments are planned and subject to mission approval, licensing and export rules.

What we provide, what you bring

We provide

  • Compute on Heleo Mesh, with research credits for selected labs
  • Access to the Heleo-1 ground twin
  • Engineering help to port and package your workload
  • Telemetry from orbital runs: power, temperature and error counts

You bring

  • A clear research question and a plan to publish
  • Your models, code and data
  • A named lead researcher at your institution
  • Feedback on what works and what does not

Your team leads the work, owns its data and keeps the credit. Results that use Heleo Mesh or Heleo-1 are shared and cited, so the whole field moves faster.

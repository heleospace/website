Research · Field 04

A live network from the ground to orbit

Heleo Mesh links ground nodes today and Heleo-1 next. Use it to study how networks and autonomous machines work when links come and go.

City lights from the ISS · NASA

Overview

Networks that reach orbit are never fully connected: satellites pass in and out of view, links fade and nodes fail. Heleo Mesh is built for those conditions. It routes work around gaps, keeps state across outages and lets machines settle payments between themselves.

That makes it a working research platform for communications, distributed systems and autonomy, not a simulation. You study the same software that runs the live ground network. About the Autonomous Mesh.

What you can do

Ground-to-orbit links

Study ground-station passes, laser relay links and how traffic moves between ground and orbit.

Delay- and fault-tolerant networking

Test routing and storage strategies when contact windows are short and nodes can fail.

Agents that trade and settle

Research AI agents and machines that negotiate, buy and sell compute, and settle automatically. Machine economy.

Secure coordination

Study identity, trust and decentralized coordination between machines owned by different organisations.

Research questions we want to help answer

  1. How should work be routed when contact with each node comes and goes on a fixed schedule?
  2. How can jobs keep running through link outages without losing results or duplicating work?
  3. How do payments stay correct when the network splits and later heals?
  4. How can machines from different owners trust each other enough to trade work?
  5. What do agents need to price compute fairly in a live market?

How a project runs

Every project starts on the ground and moves to orbit only when it is ready. You keep the same code and the same account at every stage.

StageStatusWhat happens
1. Ground meshAvailable nowRun experiments on the live ground mesh, including injected link and node faults.
2. Heleo-1 ground twinPlannedAdd Heleo-1 link schedules and limits to your experiments on the ground twin.
3. Heleo-1 in orbitPlannedRun your protocol or agent across ground nodes and Heleo-1 together.

Orbital access and hosted experiments are planned and subject to mission approval, licensing and export rules.

What we provide, what you bring

We provide

  • Access to the live ground mesh and its test tools
  • Mesh IDs for your agents and machines
  • Link and settlement logs for your experiments
  • Engineering help with the Mesh software, which is open

You bring

  • A protocol, algorithm or agent to test
  • A measurement plan
  • Your own nodes or robots, if you want to connect them
  • A plan to publish the results

Your team leads the work, owns its data and keeps the credit. Results that use Heleo Mesh or Heleo-1 are shared and cited, so the whole field moves faster.

Roadmap

Prove it on the ground. Then fly.

Research-led in Years 1 and 2, first orbital demonstration in Year 3, an open standard for ground-to-orbit compute by Year 5.

Star trails from the ISS · NASA

Five years, three pipelines

The Helios Mesh, the autonomous economy and our chip work advance together. Each year adds to what makes the network hard to replace. Years are targets from the start of the program, not promises.

YearHelios MeshAutonomous economyChips
1Ground testbed and lab validationSettlement design and testnetCommercial low-power module qualified
2Flight-ready engineering; pilots on the ground meshMachine-to-machine payments between partner nodesCo-design using lab and ground data
3Heleo-1 in orbitFirst machine-to-machine payments in orbitTest chip validated in orbit
4Heleo-2 to Heleo-4, inter-satellite links, commercial servicesAutonomous economy in productionCustom chip roadmap
5Open standard for ground-to-orbit computeRecurring settlement revenueCustom chip in development

Gates between phases

Before flight: measured radiation and thermal limits for the compute module, and settlement that stays correct under injected faults on the ground testbed. Before scale: Heleo-1 telemetry that confirms the lab results over a full year in orbit. Before the standard: independent groups running Helios nodes in production.

Who it serves

Universities: open-source Helios, testbed access and joint research. Government: resilient compute across ground and orbit, and sovereign deployments. Business: satellite operators, AI and cloud companies, telecoms and energy, with hosted mesh services and orbital capacity from Year 4.

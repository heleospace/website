Five years, four pipelines

The Heleo Mesh and its agents, compute supply, settlement and our chip work advance together. Each year adds to what makes the network hard to replace. Years are targets from the start of the program, not promises.

YearHeleo Mesh and agentsCompute supplySettlementChips

4Logistics and robot agents at scalePartner fleets join; Heleo-2 to Heleo-4; national (sovereign) capacityTreasury services for machinesFirst Heleospace chip in certified nodes

Gates between phases

Before flight: measured radiation and thermal limits for the compute module, and settlement that stays correct under injected faults on the ground testbed. Before scale: Heleo-1 telemetry that confirms the lab results over a full year in orbit. Before the standard: independent groups running Heleo Mesh nodes in production.

Who it serves

Universities: open-source Heleo Mesh, testbed access and joint research. Government: resilient compute across ground and orbit, and sovereign deployments. Business: satellite operators, AI and cloud companies, telecoms and energy, with agents, AI inference routing and certified ground capacity from Year 1, and orbital capacity from Year 3.

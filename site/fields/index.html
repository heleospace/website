Fields · Heleospace for Research

We support the research. You make the discovery.

Heleospace gives labs, universities and agencies compute on Heleo Mesh today, and a way into orbit through Heleo-1, our planned orbital data center. Your team leads the work and keeps the credit. We provide the platform it runs on.

Earth at night · NASA, via Unsplash

Why orbit matters for research

Reaching space is still hard for a single lab. Missions take years, rideshare slots are scarce, and the conditions are difficult to reproduce on the ground. Heleo-1, our planned orbital data center, puts compute in orbit and connects it to Heleo Mesh, so researchers can run work in real space conditions without building a mission of their own.

Real space conditions

Radiation, vacuum, heat limits and power constraints, measured rather than simulated.

Flight heritage for your work

Results from orbit carry weight in papers, grants and the next mission proposal.

Ground and orbit as one network

Develop on the ground mesh, then move the same workload to Heleo-1.

Three levels of access

Start on the ground today and move to orbit as Heleo-1 comes online.

Available now

Mesh access

Compute on the ground mesh for training, inference and simulation. Research credits are available for selected labs.

Reserve your place

Orbital access

Run your code or model on Heleo-1 in orbit. Develop first on the Heleo-1 ground twin, a testbed that mirrors the orbital system.

By invitation

Orbital partner

A hosted experiment slot on a Heleospace mission, first access to orbital datasets, and joint mission recognition.

Orbital access and hosted experiments are planned and subject to mission approval, licensing and export rules.

Field 01

AI & model training

Train, fine-tune and run models on Heleo Mesh. Work is routed to the compute that fits it, and it settles automatically.

  • Compute for model training and inference
  • Run your model in orbit on Heleo-1
  • Study how AI behaves under radiation and power limits
  • Open-source tooling and research credits

Field 02

Earth & climate

Process Earth observation data where it is captured. Send down the answer instead of the whole image.

  • In-orbit processing for climate and environmental research
  • Faster insight for disaster response studies
  • Agriculture, oceans and maritime research
  • Ground and orbit workflows on one network

Field 03

Chips & space hardware

Space is the hardest test environment there is. Find out how your hardware and software perform in real orbit.

  • Radiation and fault behaviour of processors and memory
  • Thermal and power-efficiency studies in vacuum
  • Open orbital telemetry datasets
  • Hosted experiment slots for partner labs

Field 04

Networks & autonomy

A live network from Earth to orbit for research on communications and autonomous systems, with machines that trade machine-to-machine.

  • Ground-to-orbit and inter-satellite links
  • Delay-tolerant and fault-tolerant networking
  • AI agents that negotiate, trade and settle in orbit
  • Secure, decentralized coordination between machines

Field 05

Engineering & simulation

Heavy simulation when you need it, with no cluster to buy or run.

  • Simulation and digital twins
  • Design and verification workloads
  • Burst compute for deadlines and grant cycles
  • Pay only for the work that runs

AI compute is outgrowing the ground. New data centers wait years for grid connections and need large amounts of water, land and cooling. In orbit, sunlight is near-continuous and heat radiates directly to space.

Moving compute to orbit solves the physics. The harder problem is coordination: making orbital and ground nodes work, and settle, as one economy. That is what Heleospace builds.

## What we are building

**The Autonomous Mesh.** Heleo Mesh is our open software that routes compute, energy, data and tasks to where they run best, on Earth or in orbit.

**Settlement.** Machines agree a price and pay each other for compute, energy, bandwidth and data, automatically.

**Heleo-1.** Our reference satellite for the Mesh in orbit. It is a concept today; the first orbital demonstration is planned for Year 3.

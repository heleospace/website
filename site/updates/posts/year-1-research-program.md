The main barrier to compute in orbit is radiation, which damages or disrupts commercial chips. Our Year 1 program is designed to remove that barrier in partner labs before anything flies.

| Workstream | Output |
| --- | --- |
| Radiation characterization | Measured error and latch-up rates, total dose tolerance |
| Shielding trade study | The lightest shielding that meets the dose target |
| Fault-tolerant mesh | Recovery time and data-loss figures under injected faults |
| Thermal-vacuum testing | Verified heat limits per node |
| Settlement under stress | Proof that payments stay correct when nodes fail |

We are looking for labs with beam-test, thermal-vacuum or distributed-systems capability. [Get in touch](/contact/?topic=research).

Today, compute is bought by people, through contracts and invoices. That works when a data center sits in one place, on one grid, under one operator.

Orbital compute is different. Nodes move, links between orbit planes come and go, and capacity depends on sunlight, temperature and radiation. Scheduling that by hand does not scale.

In the Helios Mesh, a satellite with spare capacity, a ground data center short of power and a lab with a workload can find each other, agree a price and settle in seconds, with no person in the loop.

> Hardware can be bought and code can be forked. A working orbital economy, with its nodes, settlement history and relationships, cannot.

That is why we focus on the network intelligence and the settlement layer, and work with partners for chips, satellite buses and launch.

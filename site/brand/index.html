Logo files

02

Clear space

Keep the space around the logo free of text, images and page edges. On every side, leave at least x, which is half the height of the mark.

03

Minimum size

Below these sizes the logo loses its detail. When the full logo will not fit, use the mark on its own. Shown here at actual size on a standard screen.

Full logo

120 px wide on screen, 25 mm wide in print.

Mark

16 px wide on screen, 5 mm wide in print.

04

Colour and backgrounds

The logo appears only in black or white. Choose whichever stands out most clearly from the background. On photographs, place it over a calm, even area.

05

What to avoid

The logo only stays recognisable if it looks the same everywhere. Please do not change the files in any of these ways.

Typography

08

Writing about us

Heleospace is one word with a capital H. Please do not write HeleoSpace, Heleo Space or HELEOSPACE.

NameWhat it refers to
HeleospaceThe company, in everyday use
Heleospace LLCThe legal name, for contracts and formal notices
Heleo MeshThe product: our open software and network. Always two words, never Heleo alone.
The Autonomous MeshThe idea behind Heleo Mesh, from our technical paper
Heleo-1 to Heleo-4The reference satellites

Short description

Heleospace builds orbital data centers that run as an autonomous economy: compute in orbit and on the ground that finds work, agrees a price and settles on its own.
09

Terms of use

The Heleospace name and logo belong to Heleospace LLC, which keeps all rights in them.

You may

  • Use the unmodified logo to refer to Heleospace or its work, for example in articles, reports, talks and directories.
  • Link the logo to heleo.space.

Without our written permission, you may not

  • Use the name or logo in a way that suggests we sponsor, endorse or work with you.
  • Use them on merchandise or anything you sell.
  • Use them, or anything similar, in your own name, logo, product or domain.

We may ask you to change or stop a use of the name or logo. For permission, or for a format that is not here, write to hello@heleo.space.

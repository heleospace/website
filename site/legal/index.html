Privacy

This site stores no form data itself. Messages sent through the contact form are emailed directly to our inbox from our own server and are not stored on this site. Cookies and analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site, for example which pages are read and how people arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and sends usage data, including a shortened IP address, device and browser information, to Google. We use it only to improve the site, not for advertising, and we do not combine it with other data.

Google Analytics loads only if you choose "Accept" in the cookie banner. If you choose "Deny", no analytics cookies are set. We remember your choice in your browser's local storage for 12 months. You can change it at any time through Cookie settings, and you can delete cookies in your browser settings. Google's own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.
